Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026 (account deletion)

This policy explains what information the FSM (Fantasy Stock Market) app collects, why, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. FSM is a free fantasy game that follows the real stock market. We only collect what the game needs to work.

1. What we collect

Your account

  • Username, display name, first and last name, email address and date of birth.
  • Your password, stored only as a secure one-way hash (we can't read it).
  • If you sign in with Apple or Google: the account identifier and the email address they share with us. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.
  • The version of the terms and game rules you accepted, and whether you agreed to marketing messages.
  • Your invite code and, if you used one, who invited you.

Your profile photo (optional). The server turns it into a 512 x 512 image and removes hidden details such as location and camera data. Other players can see it, and can report it.

Your game activity: your cards, game points and points history, leagues and picks, the weekly challenge, daily calls, lessons, missions, badges, XP and levels.

Players you follow and block: who you follow, who follows you, and who you blocked. Other players can see your follower and following counts and lists; nobody is told that you blocked them.

Chat messages: the messages you send in league chats and direct chats, when you sent them, and what you have read. Messages are text only. They are seen by the players in that chat, and by our team only when a player reports a message, so we can decide whether to keep or remove it. Your reports of other players' messages are kept too.

Purchases of game points (if any): Apple sends us a signed record of the purchase (transaction number, product and date) so we can add the points. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card or bank details.

Your device: if you allow notifications, the push token Apple gives your phone, plus the app version, time zone and language, so we can send notifications at the right time. We also remember which accounts a phone was used with, only to stop invite rewards being collected twice on one phone.

Technical records: when the app talks to our server we record the time, the address it came from (IP address), the app and device type, what was requested and whether it worked. Full details are kept only for errors and actions such as sign-ins, buys and sells.

2. How we use it

  • To create and run your account and sign you in safely.
  • To run the game: value your cards, rank leagues and leaderboards, and give rewards.
  • To show your username, display name, photo, level and trophies to other players on leaderboards and in leagues.
  • To send the notifications you allow (for example league results, daily call results and stock alerts). You can turn each kind off in the app.
  • To deliver chat messages to the players in a chat, and to review messages players report.
  • To keep FSM safe: prevent abuse and cheating (such as fake invites), and find and fix problems.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Sign in with Google

If you sign in with Google, we receive only your Google account identifier, your name and your email address. We use them only to create your FSM account and sign you in. We do not share them with anyone, and we do not use them for advertising. FSM's use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. Who else handles it

We share information only with the services that run FSM for us, and only what they need:

  • Microsoft Azure hosts our servers and database, in Sweden (European Union).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications and in-app purchases.
  • Google: Sign in with Google.

Real market prices come from a market data provider. No personal information is sent to it.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your account and game activity: while your account exists.
  • Technical records: 14 days.
  • Notifications in the app (the inbox): 30 days.
  • Chat messages: 180 days. A message you delete loses its text straight away, unless it was reported (then we keep it until we have reviewed it). A reported message waiting for review is kept until then.
  • A replaced or removed profile photo is deleted within a day.

Closing your account. You can delete your account at any time in the app (profile menu › Delete account). When you do, we straight away:

  • delete your email address, first and last name, date of birth, profile photo, password, Apple and Google sign-in links, sign-in history, phones, notification settings and inbox;
  • end every session, and ask Apple to revoke FSM's access if you used Sign in with Apple;
  • remove your follows, blocks and invite code, take you out of leagues that haven't started, and cancel the ones you created;
  • delete your chat messages (a message someone reported is kept until we have reviewed it), and close your cards and points.

What stays, without your name: your results in leagues that already started or ended (shown as "Deleted player"), anonymous game records, records of point purchases we must keep, and the record of which terms you accepted. Technical records are deleted after 14 days as usual. We also keep which phones were used with the account, only to stop invite rewards being claimed again.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can change your profile details and photo, and turn notifications on or off, in the app.
  • You can delete your account yourself in the app at any time (see section 5).
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing us. We answer within 30 days.

7. Children

FSM is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Connections to our servers are encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are stored only as secure hashes, and access to our servers is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about your information: diaa.mohammed@pedmas-it.com

FSM is a game for entertainment and learning. Game points have no cash value and nothing in FSM is investment advice.
