Game rules

Version 2026-11-20 · Last updated: 20 November 2026

FSM is free to play. No purchase is needed to play, join a league or take part in any challenge. Everything you can win in FSM is game points, trophies and badges, which have no cash value. Nothing in FSM is investment advice.

Apple is not a sponsor of, and is not involved in, any contest, league or challenge in FSM.

1. Points and cards

  • New players get starting game points and a starter pack of free cards.
  • Each card follows a real stock. You buy a card with points, at the cost shown in the app at that moment.
  • A card's value moves with the real stock's percentage move since you bought it. Card types (such as Paper, Diamond, Stonks and Apes) move by different amounts and can have a sell fee or a lock period; each type's terms are shown in the app before you buy, and a card keeps the terms it was bought with.
  • Selling a card turns its current value, less any fee, back into points. Buys and sells use the latest price we have; prices may be a little delayed.

2. Leagues

  • Any player can create a public or private league; official leagues are run by FSM. Private leagues are joined with their code or by asking the creator.
  • Each player picks their cards within the league's budget before the league starts. Cards are locked from the start until the end.
  • A league runs over whole market days. Players are ranked by how their league cards perform from the start to the end, using the market's closing prices.
  • A league needs at least 2 players to start, otherwise it is cancelled. The top players get trophies; official leagues also give game-point prizes, as shown on the league.
  • Some players are FSM rivals: players run by FSM, shown with an "FSM rival" tag. They join and create public leagues, play the Weekly and Daily Challenge, and post short messages in league chats, under the same rules as everyone else, so they can also win trophies and prizes. They never join private leagues and don't reply to private messages.

3. Weekly Challenge and Daily Challenge

  • Weekly Challenge: an official league for each market week, with a rule twist shown in the app. Players are placed in groups (friends together where possible) and ranked within their group. Prizes are game points, as shown in the app.
  • Daily Challenge: on market days you pick stocks for a target shown in the app. Picks open an hour before the market opens and close as shown. If your picks meet the target at the close, you win the game points shown.

4. Invites

When a friend joins with your invite and finishes their first lesson or league, you both get the game points shown in the app, up to the limit shown. One reward per real person and phone.

5. Fair play

  • One account per person. Players may not use bots, scripts, fake accounts or shared accounts. FSM rivals, run by FSM, are the only computer players.
  • Don't use bugs, data errors or delays to gain points or prizes; please report them instead.
  • If a price or result is wrong (for example a data error or a stock split), we may correct it, and with it the cards, rankings and prizes it affected.
  • We may remove points, prizes, trophies or accounts gained by breaking these rules or the terms of use, and our decision on results is final.

6. Changes

We may change these rules, card types, prizes and challenges. Leagues and challenges that have already started keep the rules they started with, unless a change is needed to fix an error.
