FSM · Fantasy Stock Market
Collect stock cards. Play the real market. Risk nothing.

Learn the stock market by playing it

FSM is a free fantasy game for iPhone that follows the real stock market. Every stock is a card you collect with game points. When the real stock moves, your card's value moves with it, so you see how the market behaves without putting real money at risk.

What you can do

  • Follow real prices of thousands of companies, live while the market is open.
  • Collect stock cards with game points and watch them gain or lose value with the real stock.
  • Join leagues and the weekly challenge, and compete with friends.
  • Learn with short lessons, quizzes, a daily up-or-down call, missions and badges.

Just a game

FSM is for entertainment and learning only. Game points have no cash value and can never be cashed out or exchanged for money. There are no cash prizes. Nothing in FSM is investment advice.
